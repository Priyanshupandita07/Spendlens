# ECONOMICS.md

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. A typical deal:
- Company spending $500/mo on Cursor → buys $500/mo in Cursor credits through
  Credex at a 20–30% discount
- Credex margin: ~15% on credits sold
- Average contract: $500/mo × 12 months = $6,000/yr revenue per customer
- Credex gross profit per customer: ~$900/yr (15% margin)

For high-savings leads (>$500/mo identified savings):
- These companies are spending $500–$5,000/mo on AI tools at retail
- Converting them to Credex credits saves them money AND generates recurring
  revenue for Credex
- Conservative average contract value (ACV): $3,000/yr
- Credex gross profit per converted customer: ~$450/yr

**A converted lead is worth approximately $450/yr in gross profit to Credex.**

## Conversion Funnel

```
Landing page visitors         → 100%
Start audit (click CTA)       → 35%   (industry avg for free tools)
Complete audit (submit form)  → 60%   of starters = 21% of visitors
Email capture                 → 25%   of completers = 5.25% of visitors
Credex consultation booked    → 15%   of high-savings email captures
Credit purchase               → 40%   of consultations booked
```

For every 1,000 visitors:
- 210 complete an audit
- 53 give their email
- ~20 are high-savings leads (>$500/mo savings)
- 3 book a consultation
- 1.2 become paying Credex customers

Revenue per 1,000 visitors: 1.2 × $3,000 ACV = **$3,600**

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

**Target:** $1M ARR = ~333 paying customers at $3,000 ACV

**Month 1–3: Validation (0 → 50 customers)**
- Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News
- Target: 5,000 total audit completions
- 250 email captures, 50 high-savings leads, 8 consultations, 3 customers
- ARR at end of Month 3: ~$9,000

**Month 4–6: Growth (50 → 100 customers)**
- Credex customer base email campaign
- SEO content: "AI tool cost comparison 2025" articles
- Target: 500 audits/month, 25 leads/month, 8 customers/month
- ARR at end of Month 6: ~$90,000

**Month 7–12: Scale (100 → 250 customers)**
- Add benchmark mode ("your spend vs companies your size")
- Embed widget for AI tool review sites
- Referral program: share your audit, both parties get Credex credit
- Target: 1,500 audits/month, 75 leads/month, 20 customers/month
- ARR at end of Month 12: ~$330,000

**Month 13–18: Compound (250 → 333 customers)**
- Paid LinkedIn/X ads with positive ROI proven
- Partner with AI tool review sites (G2, Capterra) for lead sharing
- Target: 3,000 audits/month, 150 leads/month, 30 customers/month
- ARR at end of Month 18: ~$1,000,000

**What must be true for this to work:**
1. Audit completion rate stays above 50% (product must be genuinely useful)
2. Email capture rate stays above 20% (value shown before ask)
3. High-savings lead rate stays above 30% of email captures
4. Credex consultation close rate above 40%
5. Organic distribution generates >2,000 audits/month by Month 9

The biggest risk is audit quality — if the recommendations feel generic or
wrong, users won't share and the viral loop breaks. The audit engine's
defensibility (all numbers traced to vendor URLs) is the core moat.
