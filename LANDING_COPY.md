## FAQ — 5 Real Q&As

**Q1: Is this actually free?**
Yes, completely. No credit card, no trial, no freemium wall. The audit is free
because SpendLens is a product built by Credex — we make money if you decide
to buy discounted AI credits through us, not by charging you for the audit.

**Q2: How accurate is the pricing data?**
Every price in the audit engine is sourced from official vendor pricing pages,
with the URL and date recorded in our public PRICING_DATA.md. We update it
weekly. If you spot an error, email us and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

**Q3: Do you store my data?**
Your audit inputs are saved in your browser's localStorage so you can come back
and edit them. If you enter your email to receive the report, we store that
securely in our database. The shareable URL strips your email and company name
— it only shows tools and savings numbers.

**Q4: Is this just going to tell me to buy Credex credits?**
No. The audit engine has no bias toward Credex. If you're spending well, it
says so. Credex is only surfaced for users with >$500/mo in savings
opportunities, where discounted credits are a genuine option worth mentioning.
We'd rather give you an honest audit than a fake one that manufactures savings.

**Q5: What AI tools does it cover?**
Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API direct, OpenAI API
direct, Gemini, and Windsurf. We're adding more tools based on user requests —
submit yours via the feedback link at the bottom of your audit.
